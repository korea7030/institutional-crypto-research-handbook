CONTRIBUTING.md

Contributing Guide

Thank you for your interest in contributing to The Institutional Crypto Research Handbook.

This repository values quality over quantity.

Every contribution should improve the reliability, clarity, and long-term usefulness of the knowledge base.

⸻

Guiding Principles

Contributors are expected to:

* Prioritize evidence over opinion.
* Cite primary sources whenever possible.
* Distinguish facts from interpretations.
* Welcome constructive criticism.
* Revise conclusions when stronger evidence becomes available.

⸻

Types of Contributions

The project accepts contributions in the following categories:

* New chapters
* Concept improvements
* Historical corrections
* Additional references
* Case studies
* Diagram improvements
* Typographical fixes
* Translation improvements
* Metadata enhancements

⸻

Contribution Workflow

1. Open an Issue (optional for larger changes)
2. Discuss significant architectural or conceptual changes
3. Prepare the document following STYLE_GUIDE.md
4. Apply the RESEARCH_STANDARD.md
5. Self-review using the Quality Checklist
6. Submit for review
7. Address reviewer feedback
8. Merge after approval

⸻

Evidence Requirements

Every significant claim must:

* Be traceable.
* Include appropriate citations.
* Include an Evidence Confidence Level (ECL).
* Distinguish facts from interpretations.

Unsupported assertions will not be accepted.

⸻

Writing Standards

Every contribution must follow:

* STYLE_GUIDE.md
* RESEARCH_STANDARD.md
* RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md
* RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md

⸻

Contributor Responsibilities

Contributors should:

* Remain intellectually honest.
* Avoid overstating conclusions.
* Explicitly acknowledge uncertainty.
* Respect alternative interpretations when supported by evidence.

⸻

Final Principle

Contributors are not expected to be right.

They are expected to improve the quality of collective understanding.
