## 1. Learning Objectives

- Identify good automation targets.
- Separate process automation from analytical judgment.
- Design safer automated monitoring loops.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` structures research as a repeatable sequence with clear operational stages.[^ref-rp]

`AI_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md` similarly defines a staged workflow from scope through source collection, cross-verification, draft, review, and publication.[^ref-aiag]

These staged workflows imply natural automation targets:

- data refresh,
- alerting,
- journal templates,
- evidence-ledger population,
- dashboard updates.

## 3. What to Automate

Good candidates:

- recurring data pulls,
- threshold alerts,
- document scaffolding,
- stale-thesis reminders,
- source freshness checks.

## 4. What Not to Automate Blindly

- final probability judgment,
- conflict resolution across contradictory sources,
- thesis invalidation without review,
- ethics-sensitive publication decisions.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- Automation should reduce manual friction, not reduce epistemic discipline.
- The more consequential the judgment, the less acceptable fully unattended automation becomes.
