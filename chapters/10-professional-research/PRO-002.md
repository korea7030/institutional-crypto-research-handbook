## 1. Learning Objectives

- Design dashboards around research questions.
- Separate raw metrics from interpretation.
- Build monitoring views that surface regime change.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` requires observations to describe measurable reality without interpretation.[^ref-rs]

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` requires every task to begin with observable facts and validated evidence before synthesis.[^ref-rp]

Research dashboards should therefore do three things well:

- surface relevant observations,
- preserve metric traceability,
- and make interpretive discipline easier rather than harder.

## 3. Design Principles

- organize by question, not by vendor taxonomy,
- show definitions and update frequency,
- separate absolute levels from changes and anomalies,
- attach monitoring thresholds to explicit hypotheses.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- filling dashboards with metrics that answer no live question,
- treating dashboards as conclusions,
- hiding methodology differences across datasets.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- A dashboard is a question-support tool, not a substitute for research judgment.
- Fewer well-scoped panels often outperform a noisy all-metrics cockpit.
