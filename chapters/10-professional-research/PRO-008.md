## 1. Learning Objectives

- Identify common research biases.
- Map biases to workflow controls.
- Reduce hindsight, confirmation, and narrative bias.

## 2. Executive Summary

The internal methodology documents do not use bias theory terminology heavily, but they are clearly designed to counter known bias classes.

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` addresses overconfidence by requiring explicit unknowns and fair presentation of competing explanations.[^ref-rs]

`RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` and `RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` counter confirmation bias through dedicated counter-evidence and alternative-hypothesis stages.[^ref-rf][^ref-rp]

## 3. High-Frequency Biases

- confirmation bias,
- hindsight bias,
- anchoring,
- narrative fallacy,
- recency bias,
- outcome bias.

## 4. Workflow Countermeasures

- counter-evidence sections,
- hypothesis ranking,
- timestamped journals,
- explicit invalidation criteria,
- post-investment reviews.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- Bias control is a process design problem as much as a mindset problem.
- Strong workflows make good behavior easier under stress.
