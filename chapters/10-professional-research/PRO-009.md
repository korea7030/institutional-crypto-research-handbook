## 1. Learning Objectives

- Define core ethical obligations in research.
- Explain why uncertainty disclosure matters.
- Preserve attribution, traceability, and review integrity.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` centers intellectual honesty, evidence before opinion, and explicit handling of unknowns.[^ref-rs]

`REVIEW_PROCESS.md` defines review as an integrity-preserving process rather than a cosmetic one.[^ref-rev]

`AI_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md` forbids invented references and requires grounded, verifiable output.[^ref-aiag]

Research ethics in this handbook therefore means:

- do not overstate what the evidence supports,
- do not hide uncertainty,
- do not blur fact and interpretation,
- do not present unverified material as verified research.

## 3. Core Ethical Duties

- source honesty,
- uncertainty disclosure,
- conflict awareness,
- fair treatment of counter evidence,
- reproducibility support.

## 4. Common Ethical Failure Modes

- backfilling citations after narrative decisions,
- suppressing contrary evidence,
- laundering speculative claims through confident language,
- using AI-generated references without verification.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- Research credibility compounds slowly and breaks quickly.
- Ethical discipline is not separate from alpha generation; it is what keeps the process usable over time.
