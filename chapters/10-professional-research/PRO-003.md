## 1. Learning Objectives

- Explain the function of research journals.
- Preserve timestamped reasoning.
- Improve review and revision quality.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` states that research is iterative and conclusions may change when better evidence becomes available.[^ref-rs]

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` ends research with monitoring and revision rather than publication alone.[^ref-rp]

Research journals make this iterative model operational by preserving:

- original observations,
- contemporaneous hypotheses,
- evidence sets,
- and later revisions.

## 3. What a Good Journal Captures

- date and trigger,
- key observation,
- questions asked,
- evidence collected,
- working hypotheses,
- confidence at the time,
- invalidation criteria.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- writing only after outcomes are known,
- recording conclusions without the process that produced them,
- failing to log what was uncertain at the time.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- Journals are calibration infrastructure.
- If process memory is weak, hindsight bias becomes stronger than learning.
