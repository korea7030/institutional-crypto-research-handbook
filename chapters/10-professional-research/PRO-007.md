## 1. Learning Objectives

- Define safe roles for AI in research.
- Preserve evidence-first discipline under AI assistance.
- Design review layers that catch confident fabrication and source drift.

## 2. Executive Summary

`AI_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md` defines AI-assisted document production as a research workflow grounded in verifiable evidence, cross-verification, claim extraction, and staged review.[^ref-aiag]

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` requires evidence before opinion and forbids manufacturing certainty when evidence is incomplete.[^ref-rs]

`REVIEW_PROCESS.md` requires technical, evidence, editorial, and institutional review before a document can be marked reviewed.[^ref-rev]

The operational implication is that AI should be used to accelerate:

- discovery,
- structuring,
- consistency checking,
- and drafting,

but never as a replacement for source verification and judgment.

## 3. Safe AI Roles

- outline generation,
- claim-ledger drafting,
- citation formatting,
- cross-reference checks,
- coverage-gap detection.

## 4. Unsafe AI Patterns

- treating plausible prose as evidence,
- accepting unverified references,
- skipping adversarial review because the draft reads well.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- AI increases throughput fastest where work is structured and reviewable.
- The central risk is not that AI is useless. It is that AI can sound complete before the evidence is complete.
