## 1. Learning Objectives

- Structure a repeatable research day.
- Separate intake, investigation, synthesis, and review.
- Reduce reactive research drift.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` defines a standard research workflow of observe, classify, investigate, validate, challenge, synthesize, communicate, monitor, and revise.[^ref-rp]

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` adds that evidence must precede opinion and that research remains iterative rather than terminal.[^ref-rs]

A daily workflow should therefore be built around:

- disciplined observation,
- prioritized investigation,
- evidence validation,
- and explicit revision loops.

## 3. Practical Structure

A workable daily cycle typically contains:

1. observation intake,
2. event classification,
3. question formation,
4. targeted evidence collection,
5. short synthesis or dashboard update,
6. review of open theses and monitoring triggers.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- spending the entire day consuming feeds,
- writing conclusions before source validation,
- confusing market motion with research significance.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- A good workflow optimizes decision quality, not information volume.
- Daily research should create durable artifacts, not only temporary awareness.
