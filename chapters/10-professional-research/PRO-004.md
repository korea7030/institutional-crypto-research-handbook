## 1. Learning Objectives

- Track live theses explicitly.
- Separate thesis status from market outcome.
- Maintain invalidation and monitoring rules.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` requires conclusions to summarize observation, supporting evidence, counter evidence, remaining unknowns, and confidence level.[^ref-rf]

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` requires hypotheses to include evidence, counter evidence, assumptions, confidence, and evidence confidence level.[^ref-rp]

Thesis management turns those requirements into an ongoing operational system.

## 3. Minimum Thesis Record

Each live thesis should track:

- statement of the view,
- supporting evidence,
- counter evidence,
- key assumptions,
- probability/confidence,
- invalidation criteria,
- next monitoring items.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- keeping theses only in memory,
- changing thesis terms after the fact,
- confusing position ownership with thesis validity.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- A thesis is a managed object, not a mood.
- Explicit invalidation rules reduce attachment and improve update speed.
