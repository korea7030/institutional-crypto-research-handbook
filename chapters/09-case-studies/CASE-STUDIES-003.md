## 1. Observation

In 2020, DeFi moved from a narrow builder ecosystem into a capital-intensive onchain financial sector.

## 2. Key Dates

- June 2020: Compound governance and COMP distribution became a template for governance-token incentives in DeFi.[^ref-comp-gov]
- September 16, 2020: Uniswap announced UNI and its initial liquidity mining program.[^ref-uni-uni]
- September 18, 2020 to November 17, 2020: Uniswap's initial liquidity mining program ran across four pools.[^ref-uni-uni]

## 3. Event Structure

DeFi Summer combined:

- yield incentives,
- governance-token distribution,
- permissionless market access,
- and recursive capital reuse.

This made DeFi both economically productive and structurally reflexive.

## 4. Why It Mattered

The period proved that onchain capital formation could bootstrap liquidity extremely quickly. It also showed that incentives could manufacture growth that looked durable while remaining dependent on reward schedules.

## 5. Lessons

- Incentives can create real network effects and artificial ones at the same time.
- Governance tokens changed user behavior by making usage itself part of capital allocation.
- DeFi growth should be separated into organic usage, subsidized usage, and recursive usage.

## 6. Institutional Thinking

- DeFi Summer was not just hype. It was a live demonstration of programmable financial coordination.
- But much of the growth was incentive-sensitive and therefore path-dependent.
