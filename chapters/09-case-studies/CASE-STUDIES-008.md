## 1. Observation

Memecoin cycles repeatedly turn internet-native attention into tradeable liquidity and community identity.

## 2. Source Context

Dogecoin Foundation materials describe Dogecoin's history as beginning in 2014 and the Foundation's re-invigoration in 2021.[^ref-doge]

Shiba Inu positions itself as an ecosystem that moved from a meme experiment into a broader application and community stack.[^ref-shib]

## 3. Event Structure

Memecoin cycles usually combine:

- social virality,
- low-friction speculative entry,
- strong community identity,
- rapidly reflexive price action.

## 4. Why It Mattered

Memecoin cycles showed that crypto markets do not price only utility or cash flow. They also price:

- community coordination,
- symbolic identity,
- and viral distribution.

## 5. Lessons

- Attention can be a real market input even when fundamental utility is weak.
- Community strength can support liquidity longer than skeptics expect.
- Conversion from meme to ecosystem is possible, but not guaranteed.

## 6. Institutional Thinking

- Memecoin cycles are not noise to dismiss. They are evidence that narrative can function as capital formation infrastructure.
- But narrative durability and utility durability are not the same variable.
