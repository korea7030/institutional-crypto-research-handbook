## 1. Observation

Ethereum transitioned from proof-of-work to proof-of-stake in September 2022 without replacing the application layer.

## 2. Key Dates

- August 12, 2022: the Ethereum Foundation's "Finalized no. 36" set out the merge sequence and targeted a September 15, 2022 date.[^ref-ef-final36]
- August 24, 2022: the Ethereum Foundation announced Bellatrix for September 6, 2022 and described the Merge path to mainnet.[^ref-ef-merge]
- September 15, 2022: the Merge completed on Ethereum mainnet.[^ref-ef-merge]

## 3. Event Structure

The Merge changed the consensus engine while preserving:

- accounts,
- smart contracts,
- balances,
- and application state.

This was a rare live-system migration in which the settlement environment persisted while the security process changed.

## 4. Why It Mattered

The event changed how Ethereum was analyzed:

- from miner economics to validator economics,
- from PoW issuance narratives to staking and burn narratives,
- from energy criticism to scaling-through-rollups narratives.

## 5. Lessons

- Large protocol transitions can be executed incrementally if client diversity and testing discipline are strong.
- Consensus changes can matter economically even when users experience little application-level discontinuity.
- Ethereum's post-Merge identity became more tightly linked to staking and L2 scaling.

## 6. Institutional Thinking

- The Merge was not just a technical upgrade. It changed the investable framing of Ethereum.
- Researchers should separate execution continuity from deep changes in monetary and security mechanics.
