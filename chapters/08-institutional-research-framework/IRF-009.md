## 1. Learning Objectives

- Build a post-investment review loop.
- Separate bad outcomes from bad process.
- Revise methodology using observed mistakes.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` and `RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` both emphasize that research is iterative and remains revisable as better evidence becomes available.[^ref-rs][^ref-rf]

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` ends the research workflow with monitoring and revision rather than publication alone.[^ref-rp]

`REVIEW_PROCESS.md` frames review as a process designed to strengthen research integrity and future reliability.[^ref-rev]

Institutional research therefore does not stop when a position is opened. A serious process reviews:

- what was known at entry,
- what changed,
- what was missed,
- whether sizing matched uncertainty,
- and whether the thesis failed, timing failed, or risk control failed.

## 3. Review Structure

Post-investment review should separate:

- thesis quality,
- evidence quality,
- execution quality,
- risk management quality,
- outcome variance unrelated to process.

## 4. Common Errors

- judging process only by PnL,
- rewriting memory after the fact,
- failing to document what would have invalidated the original thesis.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- Good process can lose money in the short run.
- Bad process can make money by accident.
- The objective of review is process improvement, not ego protection.
