## 1. Learning Objectives

- Define counter evidence operationally.
- Build falsification questions.
- Reduce confirmation bias in research writing.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` requires intellectual honesty when evidence is incomplete and requires competing explanations to be presented fairly.[^ref-rs]

`RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` explicitly states that professional researchers actively search for evidence that disproves their preferred explanation.[^ref-rf]

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` formalizes this as a dedicated challenge phase and asks: what evidence weakens this explanation, which assumptions remain unverified, and could another hypothesis explain the same observation?[^ref-rp]

## 3. Why Counter Evidence Matters

Without counter evidence, research quality decays into narrative assembly. The strongest-looking conclusion may simply be the one whose contrary data was never sought.

## 4. Practical Workflow

For each hypothesis, ask:

- what would make this wrong,
- what evidence points elsewhere,
- what structural break makes past patterns unreliable.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- Counter evidence is not a courtesy to skepticism. It is core reliability infrastructure.
- A conclusion that survives challenge deserves more weight than one that merely sounds coherent.
