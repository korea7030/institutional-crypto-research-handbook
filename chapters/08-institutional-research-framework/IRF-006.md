## 1. Learning Objectives

- Translate hypotheses into scenarios.
- Distinguish base, upside, and downside cases.
- Connect scenario logic to monitoring variables.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` frames research as probability-based rather than certainty-based work and emphasizes that conclusions remain revisable.[^ref-rf]

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` pushes researchers to synthesize evidence into structured hypotheses with confidence rather than binary answers.[^ref-rp]

Scenario analysis is the natural extension: instead of one future, researchers specify several plausible futures and the conditions under which each becomes more likely.

## 3. Scenario Structure

Each scenario should include:

- core thesis,
- trigger conditions,
- confirming evidence,
- disconfirming evidence,
- portfolio implication.

## 4. Institutional Thinking

- Scenarios convert uncertainty into structured monitoring.
- The point is not to guess the future exactly. It is to be less surprised by it.
