## 1. Learning Objectives

- Build explicit hypotheses from evidence.
- Keep observation and explanation separate.
- Compare multiple explanations without forced certainty.

## 2. Executive Summary

`RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` states that professional researchers compare hypotheses rather than defend them.[^ref-rf]

`RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` requires synthesis into hypotheses containing observation, evidence, counter evidence, assumptions, confidence, and evidence confidence level.[^ref-rp]

The institutional standard is not to produce one elegant answer quickly. It is to structure several plausible explanations and rank them according to evidentiary support.

## 3. Hypothesis Structure

A useful hypothesis packet contains:

- observation,
- proposed explanation,
- supporting evidence,
- counter evidence,
- explicit assumptions,
- confidence level.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- treating hypothesis as conclusion,
- hiding assumptions,
- collapsing multiple mechanisms into one vague story.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- A well-formed hypothesis is falsifiable, conditional, and revisable.
- Multiple ranked hypotheses usually outperform one confident story.
