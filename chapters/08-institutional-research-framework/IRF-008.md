## 1. Learning Objectives

- Connect research quality to exposure size.
- Distinguish thesis strength from sizing discipline.
- Build smaller positions when uncertainty or liquidity risk is high.

## 2. Executive Summary

Phase 8 in the roadmap defines position sizing as a downstream research skill, not a separate gambling instinct. `RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md` and `RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` imply that probability, counter evidence, monitoring, and revision all feed decision quality.[^ref-rf][^ref-rp]

Position sizing is therefore the translation layer between research and capital at risk.

## 3. Sizing Inputs

At minimum, sizing should reflect:

- evidence quality,
- scenario dispersion,
- downside asymmetry,
- liquidity,
- correlation with existing positions,
- ability to revise.

## 4. Common Errors

- sizing from excitement rather than evidence,
- treating a strong narrative as a strong expected-value profile,
- ignoring market structure and exit liquidity.

## 5. Institutional Thinking

- Good research can still justify a small position.
- Sizing is where humility becomes observable.
