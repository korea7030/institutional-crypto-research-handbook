Mission

The Institutional Crypto Research Handbook is an open-source knowledge base designed to help readers develop the analytical thinking used by professional crypto researchers and institutional investors.

This project does not aim to teach readers how to memorize blockchain concepts or investment indicators.

Instead, it focuses on helping readers answer a more important question:

How should a professional researcher think when interpreting blockchain data?

⸻

Vision

Anyone can access blockchain data.

Few people know how to interpret it.

Even fewer can transform evidence into investment decisions.

This handbook exists to bridge that gap.

Our long-term vision is to build the most rigorous, evidence-based, institutional-grade open-source handbook for crypto research.

⸻

Why This Handbook Exists

Most educational resources explain:

* What Bitcoin is
* What Ethereum is
* What an ERC-20 token is
* What MVRV means
* What Exchange Reserve means

Very few explain:

* Why those concepts exist
* Which assumptions they rely on
* When they fail
* How professional researchers validate them
* How institutional investors combine multiple sources of evidence

This handbook focuses on research thinking rather than memorization.

⸻

Philosophy

The goal of this project is not to produce better traders.

The goal is to produce better researchers.

Professional research is built upon evidence, skepticism, and continuous verification.

Therefore, every chapter follows one principle:

Facts first. Interpretation second. Conclusions last.

⸻

Core Principles

1. Facts Over Opinions

Claims must be supported by verifiable evidence.

Opinions are clearly separated from facts.

⸻

2. Primary Sources First

Whenever possible, information should originate from primary sources.

Examples include:

* Bitcoin Whitepaper
* Bitcoin Improvement Proposals (BIPs)
* Ethereum Whitepaper
* Ethereum Foundation Documentation
* Ethereum Improvement Proposals (EIPs)
* Official protocol documentation
* Academic research papers

Secondary sources are used only for additional context.

⸻

3. Institutional Thinking

Retail investors often ask:

“Is this bullish?”

Institutional researchers ask:

* Why did this happen?
* What evidence supports this interpretation?
* What evidence contradicts it?
* Which alternative explanations exist?
* How confident can we be?

This difference in thinking is the foundation of this handbook.

⸻

4. Research Over Prediction

The objective is not predicting markets.

The objective is improving decision quality.

Research reduces uncertainty.

It never eliminates uncertainty.

⸻

5. Intellectual Honesty

When evidence is insufficient, we explicitly state:

Insufficient Evidence.

When a conclusion cannot be supported:

Unknown.

Confidence should never exceed available evidence.

What This Handbook Is NOT

This handbook is not:

* A beginner’s guide to cryptocurrency
* A trading signal service
* A technical analysis course
* Financial advice
* A collection of investment recommendations

⸻

Learning Philosophy

Understanding precedes analysis.

Analysis precedes interpretation.

Interpretation precedes decision-making.

Readers are encouraged to challenge every conclusion presented in this handbook.

⸻

Research Framework

Every chapter follows the same analytical workflow.

Observation

↓

Question

↓

Evidence

↓

Counter Evidence

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Probability Assessment

↓

Decision

↓

Review

The framework emphasizes repeatable research rather than intuition.

⸻

Source Hierarchy

Evidence is evaluated using the following priority:

Tier 1

* Official documentation
* Standards (BIPs / EIPs)
* Whitepapers

Tier 2

* Peer-reviewed academic papers

Tier 3

* Protocol source code

Tier 4

* Institutional research

Tier 5

* Community research

Whenever multiple sources disagree, conflicting views are documented rather than hidden.
