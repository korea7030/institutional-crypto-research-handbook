Review & Quality Assurance Process

Version 1.0

⸻

Purpose

This document defines how research documents are reviewed before becoming part of the handbook.

The review process protects the integrity and consistency of the repository.

⸻

Review Stages

Draft

↓

Technical Review

↓

Evidence Review

↓

Editorial Review

↓

Approved

↓

Published

⸻

Review Categories

Technical Accuracy

* Correct terminology
* Correct protocol behavior
* Correct historical context

⸻

Evidence Review

* Primary sources verified
* Citations complete
* Evidence traceable
* ECL assigned correctly

⸻

Institutional Review

* Institutional Thinking section present
* Counter Evidence documented
* Alternative hypotheses considered

⸻

Editorial Review

* Clear writing
* Consistent terminology
* Metadata complete
* Style Guide compliance

⸻

Approval Checklist

A document may be marked Reviewed only if:

□ Technical review passed

□ Evidence review passed

□ Editorial review passed

□ Institutional review passed

□ References verified

□ Challenge questions included

□ Metadata complete

⸻

Document Status

Draft

↓

↓

↓

↓

⸻

Review Philosophy

The objective of review is not to criticize the author.

The objective is to strengthen the research.

⸻

Final Principle

Every published document should be reproducible, evidence-based, and open to future revision.
