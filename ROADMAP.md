The Institutional Crypto Research Handbook

Learning Roadmap & Knowledge Architecture

Version: v0.1 (Foundation)

⸻

Overview

This roadmap defines the complete learning path for becoming an institutional-grade crypto researcher.

The handbook is intentionally organized from first principles toward professional research practice.

Readers are strongly encouraged to complete each phase in order.

Many advanced concepts rely on knowledge established in earlier chapters.

⸻

Learning Philosophy

This handbook follows a bottom-up approach.

Blockchain
↓
Protocols
↓
Assets
↓
Markets
↓
Behavior
↓
Research
↓
Investment Decision

The objective is not to memorize concepts.

The objective is to build a mental model that allows readers to analyze unfamiliar situations independently.

Definition of Completion

The handbook will be considered Version 1.0 when:

* All planned chapters are completed.
* All chapters have undergone evidence review.
* Primary sources are cited throughout.
* Cross-references are complete.
* Institutional Thinking sections exist in every chapter.
* Challenge Questions exist in every chapter.

Version 1.0 is not the end of the project.

It marks the beginning of continuous institutional research.
